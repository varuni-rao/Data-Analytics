# NYC Food Delivery Optimization Analysis (FoodHub)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) for **FoodHub**, a food aggregator service in New York City. The goal is to analyze customer behavior, restaurant performance, and delivery logistics to provide data-driven recommendations for business growth and operational efficiency.

## 📊 Key Insights
* **Market Scale:** Analyzed 1,898 orders across 178 restaurants and 1,200 unique customers.
* **Top Performers:** 'Shake Shack' is the high-volume leader. American and Japanese cuisines dominate the market share.
* **Service Gaps:** 38% of customers (736) have not rated their experience, representing a significant data gap for feedback loops.
* **Logistics Bottleneck:** While weekend delivery is efficient, **weekday delivery times** are significantly higher, indicating a need for better route optimization or staffing.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Customer Retention:** Implement a point-based loyalty system (e.g., free delivery after $100 spend) to encourage repeat orders.
2. **Incentivize Feedback:** Introduce small incentives (discounts/points) for ratings to improve the recommendation engine's accuracy.
3. **B2B Growth:** Offer a 25% discount on commission charges for restaurants that achieve quarterly order growth, fostering a partnership mindset.
4. **Operational Optimization:** Deploy GPS-based alternate routing for delivery personnel on weekdays to combat high traffic latency.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python
* **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib
* **Process:** Data Cleaning, Univariate/Bivariate Analysis, Insights Generation
