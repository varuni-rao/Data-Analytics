# Global Restaurant Market Intelligence (Zomato Analysis)

## 📌 Project Overview
A global analysis of restaurant performance and customer satisfaction across the Zomato ecosystem. This project focuses on the correlation between **cost, location, and rating** to help stakeholders understand market saturation and value perception.

## 🌍 Global Footprint
* **Dataset Scale:** 9,551 restaurants analyzed worldwide.
* **Consumer Rating:** Average global rating stands at **2.67**, suggesting a high level of critical feedback or market-specific rating behaviors.
* **Cost Analysis:** Average cost per order is approximately **$1.20K** (normalized across currencies), with high-end outliers in specific Asian markets.

## 🔍 Key Visualizations
* **World Wide Analysis Report:** A geospatial view of restaurant density vs. average rating.
* **Top 5 Performance Metrics:** A comparative bar chart highlighting the most expensive vs. the highest-rated restaurants to identify "Value for Money" leaders.
* **Cuisine Distribution:** Analysis of local vs. international cuisine counts across major cities.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Tool:** Power BI
* **Features:** Geospatial Mapping, Slicers for City/Country-level filtering, Advanced Data Filtering.
